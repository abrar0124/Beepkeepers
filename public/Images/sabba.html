Why frontline businesses love Beekeeper

Take the product tour

Frontline workers are the backbone of your business, but often lack the support and info they need to excel.

Ineffective communication

Traditional desk-based communication tools fail the frontline, leading to information gaps that cause errors and erode trust.

Negative experiences

Poor onboarding, unfilled shifts, and burnout impact morale. A bad deskless worker experience hurts productivity, culture, and retention.

Hard-to-access tools

Difficulty accessing critical information frustrates workers and increases safety risks, downtime, and compliance failures.

The Beekeeper advantage

The only platform built for how the frontline actually works

Total connectivity and access

Beekeeper ensures smooth communication across all levels of your organization, from HQ to the frontline, regardless of device or location, keeping your team connected and engaged.

Flexible integrations, all in one place

Integrate seamlessly with your HRIS, LMS, payroll systems, and more. With Beekeeper’s REST API and OOTB integrations available through the Marketplace, connecting your team’s favorite and business-critical tools is fast and easy.

Insightful analytics

Use Beekeeper’s AI-powered analytics and reporting tools to gain real-time insights. Track engagement, measure operational performance, and uncover trends to drive informed decisions that boost frontline productivity and reduce turnover.

Reliable performance for business continuity

Beekeeper delivers consistent performance and availability, maintaining a 99.9% uptime level. Our frontline employee app scales with your organization, handling increased usage and data easily.

Data security you can trust

Protect your sensitive workforce data with Beekeeper’s secure cloud infrastructure, role-based permissions, and full encryption. Fully compliant with GDPR and ISO 27001, your data security is our top priority.

No-fuss implementation for fast time-to-value

It’s easy to get started with Beekeeper’s frontline employee mobile app. Use the ready-to-use templates to quickly customize and launch forms, surveys, and workflows, reducing setup time and ensuring consistency.
